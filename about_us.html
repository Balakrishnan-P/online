Who We Are

DLK Technologies India Private Limited, based in Chennai, India, is a leading provider of IT solutions and high-fidelity digital marketing services. Since our founding in 2010, we’ve empowered businesses across construction, financial services, healthcare, and education with innovative, scalable solutions.

With a global presence in India and Bahrain, we specialize in bridging talent and opportunity through custom software development, digital marketing, and emerging technologies like IoT and Raspberry Pi. Our rigorous quality management ensures high-quality deliverables at every stage.

Our History

Established in 2010 in Chennai, DLK Technologies started as a small startup with a vision to simplify technology for businesses. Today, we’re a global leader, serving clients in over 10 countries with offices in India and Bahrain.

Our journey is marked by a commitment to innovation, completing over 100 projects in software development, digital marketing, and IoT solutions. We’ve built a reputation for excellence through quality assurance and client-centric approaches.
